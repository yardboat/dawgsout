# 11 · Gemini prompts: comic-panel cutscenes
The game already plays these cutscenes with stand-in art. Drop finished images into `game/assets/panels/` with the exact filenames below and they replace the stand-ins automatically. No code change needed.

**Attach both** `inbox/joby_v1_gemini.jpg` (character) and `inbox/map_v1_gemini.jpg` (world style) as references for every prompt.

## Shared block (paste first in every prompt)
A single comic-book panel, hand-inked cartoon with loose watercolor fill, matching the attached references exactly: confident black ink outlines, warm watercolor washes, slight paper texture. Landscape 4:3 framing. The character is Nasty Joby from the reference: a grumpy man with messy ginger hair whose body is a giant red-and-white beer can costume, with arms poking out of holes in the sides and legs in white socks and brown sneakers. Leave the TOP THIRD of the image visually quiet (sky, wall or plain background) so a speech bubble can sit there. **No text, no speech bubbles, no letters, no logos anywhere in the image.** The can label is plain red and white with abstract swirls, not a real beer brand.

## reveal_1.png: "What are they doing?! They're gonna ruin it!"
[shared block] Night street in a small college town, string lights and a glowing bar sign out of focus behind him. Joby is furious and panicked: eyes bulging, eyebrows up, mouth wide open mid-shout, one hand pointing off-panel to the left, the other gripping a small beer can. Medium shot from the waist up, slight low angle. Motion lines around his head.

## reveal_2.png: the pull
[shared block] Same night street. Joby tilts his head way back and chugs from the small beer can in his hand, eyes squeezed shut, foam spilling down his chin, free hand balled into a fist. Close-up, slightly from below, dramatic. Little droplets flying.

## wake_1.png: woken early, "WHAT are you DOING?!"
[shared block, but WITHOUT the beer can: Joby is in striped red pajamas] Night bedroom with a moonlit window. Joby has just bolted upright in bed, blanket flying off, hair wild, eyes bulging, mouth wide open mid-yell, pointing at the viewer. Shock lines around his head. Wide shot of the bed.

## wake_2.png: suiting up
[shared block] Same bedroom. Joby, still in pajama sleeves, is yanking the giant beer-can costume up over his body like a pair of pants, gritting his teeth, one leg still lifted. The can is halfway up, his head and furious face poking out the top. Dynamic, slightly from below.

## caught.png (optional, for the "caught" moment)
[shared block] Joby leaning right into the camera with a smug, nasty grin, raising his small beer can in a mocking toast. Extreme close-up, fish-eye feel.

## melt.png (optional, for the win)
[shared block] Joby in horror, half-melted: his face and the beer can sagging and dripping downward like wax, foam bubbling, hands on his cheeks. Mouth open in a long scream.
